## Biography

I am a Full Professor at the School of Control and Computer Engineering, North China Electric Power University (NCEPU). Prior to this, I was an Associate Professor at the State Key Laboratory of Processors (SKLP), Institute of Computing Technology (ICT), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS) from 2018 to 2026. I was a Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore from 2016 to 2018, and received my Ph.D. from The University of Hong Kong in 2016.

My research focuses on reconfigurable and reliable architectures for AI computing systems, with emphasis on large-scale spatial architectures, domain-specific accelerators, and hardware–software co-design. More recently my work has extended to LLM-driven hardware design automation, including hardware code generation, code-generation agents, and automated SoC design. I have published around 100 papers in leading international conferences and journals, and received Best Paper Awards at GLSVLSI 2021 and IEEE Transactions on Computers 2021, the CAS Outstanding Award for Technological Achievements Transformation (2019), and the Huawei Olympus Pioneering Award (2023).
